QR Code Generator — Support Page

QR Creator lets you generate QR codes for URLs, text, contacts, Wi-Fi networks, and more — entirely on your device, with no internet connection required. The optional Center Logo feature lets you place your own image in the center of any QR code.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a QR code?

Open the app, select a content type (URL, text, Wi-Fi, etc.), fill in the details, and tap Generate. Your QR code appears instantly.

How do I add a logo to my QR code?

Unlock the Center Logo feature with a one-time purchase. After unlocking, tap the logo icon on the editor screen to pick an image from your photo library. Use the size slider to adjust how large the logo appears.

Will my QR code still scan with a logo?

Yes. QR Creator uses high error-correction level (H), which allows up to 30% of the code to be obscured. As long as the logo does not exceed the recommended size, the code remains scannable.

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. All QR code generation happens on your device. No data is sent to any server.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to the Add Your Logo screen and tap Restore purchases. Your previous purchase will be re-activated using your Apple ID.

How do I save or share a QR code?

After generating a QR code, tap the Share button to save it to your photo library, copy it, or send it via any app.
