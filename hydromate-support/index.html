Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set my daily water goal?

Open HydroMate and go to Settings → Daily Goal. You can set a custom amount in ml or oz, or let the app calculate it automatically based on your body weight from Apple Health.

Why isn't HydroMate reading from Apple Health?

Go to iPhone Settings → Health → Apps → HydroMate and make sure Read & Write permissions are enabled for Body Weight and Water.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap the Settings icon in the app, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

The app shows the wrong streak — how do I fix it?

Streaks are calculated based on your logged entries. If you log water before midnight, it counts for that day. Entries can be added retroactively from the History tab.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete All Data. This will permanently remove all water logs from the app. Health data written to Apple Health must be deleted separately from the Health app.
