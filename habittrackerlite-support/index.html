Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new habit?

Tap the + button on the main screen to create a new habit. Enter a name, choose an icon and color, then set the frequency (daily, weekly, or specific days). Tap Save to start tracking.

How do I use the widgets on my Home Screen?

Long-press your iPhone Home Screen, tap + in the top corner, then search for HabitTracker Lite. Choose the widget size you prefer (small, medium, or large) and tap Add Widget. Widgets update automatically as you complete habits.

How does the Apple Watch app work?

Install HabitTracker Lite on your iPhone and the Watch app will appear automatically in the Watch app. Open it on your wrist to see today's habits and mark them complete — changes sync instantly to your iPhone.

How do I restore my Premium Lifetime purchase?

Tap the Settings icon, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase. If the issue persists, contact us below.

How are streaks calculated?

A streak counts how many consecutive days you've completed a habit. If you complete a habit before midnight it counts for that day. Missing a day resets the streak to zero. You can view your full history in the Stats tab.

How do I delete my data or account?

Go to Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete All Data. This permanently removes all habits, check-ins, and streaks from the app. Since HabitTracker Lite stores data locally on your device, no server-side deletion is required.
